05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Curious?
We like that.

Starting something new comes with questions. Here are a few of the usual ones.

What if my business is just starting?

That's a great time to start. Whether you have an idea, a Facebook page or an established business, we'll discuss what makes sense for your current stage.

Can we start with just a website?

Absolutely. We can discuss a focused website or landing page first and explore other services when they are useful to your business.

How do you use AI in your work?

AI can support research, ideas and creative drafts. The work is reviewed and adapted to the needs of your business.

Do you work with international businesses?

We welcome enquiries from Bangladesh and beyond. Project scope, communication and payment arrangements are discussed before work begins.

How much will my project cost?

Every project is different. We discuss your goals and agree on a clear quote and scope before starting.
